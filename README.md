I'm a software engineer in Mumbai. I build AI products end to end — the model pipeline, the backend, the app, and the infrastructure under them. Most of my time goes to making models behave in production, where a wrong answer is someone's dinner.

That work is mostly [NutriScan](https://nutriscan.app/), an AI nutrition app on iOS and Android with 100K+ downloads on Google Play and 4.4★ from 432 App Store ratings, shipped in 12 languages. On the side I write [DevCLI](https://github.com/NesanSelvan/devcli), a terminal built for the Claude Code era, and publish Flutter packages to pub.dev.

## Selected work

**[NutriScan](https://nutriscan.app/)** — AI nutrition tracking for iOS and Android.

Point a camera at a plate: a vision model reads the food, a language model resolves it into macros, micros and an A–E grade, streamed back over a WebSocket. Around that sit NutriBites, a retrieval assistant over your own meal history combining text-to-SQL with vector search; Monika, a voice nutritionist running natively in the app; and 28-day diet plans generated from BMR, TDEE and goal, with the grocery list to shop them. Apple Health and Google Fit sync, home-screen widgets, and over-the-air Flutter updates.

**[DevCLI](https://github.com/NesanSelvan/devcli)** — a terminal built for the agent era.

A real shell alongside a side panel for your prompt vault, agents, skills and MCP servers. Rust, Tauri and xterm. Currently v0.3.9 for macOS on Apple Silicon.

**Flutter packages** — published on pub.dev.

[wiggle_kit](https://pub.dev/packages/wiggle_kit) for one-line wiggle animations on any widget, [tooltip_pro](https://pub.dev/packages/tooltip_pro) for rich tooltips with blur, shadows and precise positioning, and [haptic_feedback_pro](https://pub.dev/packages/haptic_feedback_pro) for native haptics on iOS and Android.
